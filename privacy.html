ההגדרות (למשל אילו קורסים לבחור) ורשימת הקבצים שכבר ירדו נשמרות רק בדפדפן או במחשב של המשתמש. אפשר למחוק אותן בכל עת על ידי הסרת התוסף או התוכנה.

Moodle Sorter - Privacy Policy

In short: the app collects, stores and sends no personal data.

Moodle Sorter (a Safari and Chrome extension and a desktop program) downloads files from the Moodle site the user is already signed in to, using the browser's existing session, and organises them into folders on the user's own device. It never sees, stores or types passwords; it only reads from the Moodle site and changes nothing there; it sends no data to any other server, uses no analytics or tracking, and shows no ads. Settings and the list of already-downloaded files are kept only on the user's device and are removed with the extension or program.
